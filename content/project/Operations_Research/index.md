#### About this deck

A structured, 20-slide overview of Operations Research (OR) — the
discipline of using mathematical modeling, statistical analysis, and
algorithmic methods to arrive at optimal or near-optimal decisions in
complex, resource-constrained systems.

#### What it covers

The deck works through the full taxonomy of optimization problems
(deterministic vs. stochastic, continuous vs. combinatorial optimization,
network and flow models, inventory control, and queueing theory), the
five-stage optimization workflow from problem formulation through
implementation, and a concrete application domain — production management —
spanning manufacturing scheduling, maintenance and project scheduling, and
physical distribution and facilities planning.

#### Why it matters to my research

Operations Research supplies the formal language to turn engineering
intuition — about defect tolerance, layout efficiency, or process
parameters — into models that can be optimized, validated, and defended
quantitatively, directly complementing the manufacturing and materials work
elsewhere in this portfolio.
